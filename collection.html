Why a box, not a product

We're selling the routine.

A candle on its own is a candle. A supplement on its own is a pill. Put them in sequence — light, brew, write, soak, rest — and you have a ritual your body starts to recognise. Every Better Noum box is built around the same nightly arc, so the habit forms itself.

A wind-down playlist

Every box carries a QR code to a curated Better Noum playlist — low, warm, unhurried sound to score the last hour of the day.

One evening supplement

An all-in-one capsule with magnesium, GABA and 5-HTP — formulated to support a calming evening routine.*

The same sleep ritual

Every box shares our caffeine-free nighttime drinks and capsules — the warm, familiar heart of the routine.

* Supports a calming evening routine. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Availability subject to UAE regulatory review.
