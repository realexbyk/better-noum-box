What's inside

Six elements. One evening.

Nothing in the tray is random. Each element serves a step of the same wind-down — chosen to work together, arranged in the order of your night.

Explore the tray

Tap a number to see what each compartment holds and the step it serves.

Step one · The Cup

Every ritual begins with warmth.

Seven caffeine-free nighttime blends — one for each evening. Golden milk with turmeric and date notes, chamomile and botanical infusions, warm spice blends made for the last cup of the day.

Each sachet is a single serving: tear, brew, hold with both hands. Full ingredients printed on every sachet.

Step two · The Air

Let the room change first.

A pillow & room mist of lavender and cedar, calibrated for night — present, never perfumey. Two sprays on the pillow, one in the air, and the bedroom starts behaving like a bedroom.

Patch-test before first use. Full ingredient list on the bottle and online.

Step three · The Water

Warm water does half the work.

Single-soak sachets of magnesium-rich mineral salts with a whisper of the same night scent. One sachet, one soak — no scooping, no guessing.

No bathtub? Every ritual card includes a foot-soak version. A bowl of warm water counts.

Step four · The Close

End the day on purpose.

Seven ritual cards — each one scripts an evening in three small steps and closes with a single line worth falling asleep on. Alongside them, a silk-feel sleep mask in taupe, soft enough to forget you're wearing it.

The cards are the quiet engine of the whole box: they turn nice products into a kept routine.

Chosen, not collected

Curation is the product.

Every element is sourced for the same sensory world — warm, soft, low-lit — and tested together as one evening, not approved separately as six products. If an item doesn't earn its place in the sequence, it doesn't ship.

We don't promise sleep miracles. We design better evenings.

Coming evenings

The tray will keep evolving.

Seasonal scents. New blends. Limited Ramadan and Eid editions. Each month's tray keeps the same four steps — and finds new ways to fill them. Subscribers always see what's coming before it ships.
