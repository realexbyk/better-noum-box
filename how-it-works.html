Why monthly?

A month is the rhythm of a ritual.

Seven scripted evenings, then the freedom to repeat your favourites with what remains. Most people finish a tray in four to six weeks — so monthly keeps the box alive without the clutter of too much, too often. If your pace is slower, skip a month in two taps.
